Common Questions

Why does the score differ from live market moves?

MarketPulse is a low-frequency sentiment app. It may use public, delayed, or periodically refreshed data. It is designed for context, not real-time trading.

Is MarketPulse financial advice?

No. MarketPulse is for education and informational context only. It is not investment advice, trading advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell securities.

Why does the home-screen widget or app icon not update instantly?

In the current release, the widget updates when you tap its refresh ⟳ icon or when the app successfully refreshes Pulse data. The dynamic launcher icon updates when the app loads new Pulse. Production background push updates are not included yet.

What do the 14 indicators measure?

Tap the small ⓘ button on any indicator card inside the app to see a plain-English explanation, the healthy range, and the update frequency for that indicator.

How do I request deletion of feedback?

Email support with enough detail to identify your submission, such as approximate date, platform, and feedback text. If you attached an image, mention that too.

Response target

This is an early release. Support responses are best effort while the product is in beta and store-review preparation. We aim to reply within a few business days.
